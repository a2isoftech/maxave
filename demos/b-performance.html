01 · Cold start

Protection
from the first second.

Most engine wear happens before the oil has fully circulated. A multigrade oil flows quickly when cold to reach the cams, valves and bearings.

  • Fast oil-flow at start-up
  • Anti-wear film on metal surfaces
02 · Heat

Holds firm
at 120°C+.

Air-cooled engines run very hot in summer traffic. Stable base oils resist thinning and oxidation, so the protective film stays intact.

  • High-temperature viscosity stability
  • Oxidation inhibitors for longer oil life
03 · Clean

Keeps the
engine clean.

Detergent and dispersant additives hold carbon, soot and sludge in suspension. The dirt drains out with the old oil instead of building up inside.

  • Reduces deposit formation
  • Protects oil passages and piston rings
04 · Clutch

Crisp shifts.
No slip.

In a 4-stroke motorcycle, the same oil lubricates the gearbox and the wet clutch. Its friction is balanced so the clutch grips cleanly and gear changes stay smooth.

  • Wet-clutch compatible formulation
  • Shear-stable for gearbox protection
