Read the label

What the numbers mean

20W
The winter rating. A lower number means the oil flows faster on a cold start, which is when most engine wear happens.
50
The hot viscosity at operating temperature. A higher number gives a thicker protective film in heat and under load.
4T
Made for four-stroke engines, where one oil lubricates the engine, gearbox and wet clutch.
API / JASO
Industry performance standards. JASO MA/MA2 means the oil is safe for wet clutches. JASO MB is for scooters with an automatic (CVT) clutch.
Care guide

A better oil change
in four steps.

Most commuter bikes need fresh oil every 2,000–3,000 km. Check your owner's manual, and change it sooner if you ride in heavy dust or heat.

Warm it up

Run the engine for 2–3 minutes. Warm oil drains faster and carries more dirt out with it.

Drain completely

Remove the drain bolt and let the old oil drip out fully. Clean the strainer if your bike has one.

Fill to the mark

Refill with the quantity given in your manual (usually 0.7 L to 1 L) and check the level on the dipstick.

Note the reading

Write down your odometer reading so you know when the next change is due.

Which oil is right for my bike?

Use the Oil Finder above, or check the oil grade in your owner's manual. Most 70cc–125cc commuter bikes in Pakistan use 20W-50 4T oil.

Can I use car oil in my motorcycle?

No, this isn't recommended. Many car oils contain friction modifiers that can make a motorcycle's wet clutch slip. Always use a 4T motorcycle oil.
