Help

Frequently asked

How does Cash on Delivery work?

Place your order and we'll call to confirm it. Then pay the rider in cash when your oil arrives. There's no extra charge for COD.

How do I pay with JazzCash?

Choose JazzCash at checkout, then enter your mobile account number and the last 6 digits of your CNIC. Approve the payment request in your JazzCash app to finish.

What are the delivery charges?

Delivery is free on orders over Rs 2,000. Below that, a flat Rs 200 applies anywhere in Pakistan.

Which grade does my bike need?

Check your owner's manual. Most 70cc–125cc bikes use 20W-50. Scooters with automatic gearboxes need a JASO MB oil like Scoot 4T.

How often should I change oil?

Mineral oils: every 2,000–2,500 km. Semi-synthetic: 3,000–3,500 km. Fully synthetic: up to 5,000 km. Change sooner if you ride in dust or extreme heat.

Can I return an order?

Unopened, sealed bottles can be returned within 7 days. Call +92 304 1343605 to arrange a return.
